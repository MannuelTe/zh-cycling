Open counter data · Stadt Zürich · 2009–2026

Checking Zurich's cycling claims against its own counters

The city publishes its bike-counter data openly. We use it to test the city's public statements about cycling, and to measure how well cycling at an unmeasured location can be rebuilt from the counters that exist.

Why the scenarios differ

One counter carries the margin

Andreasstrasse's daily traffic divided by the median counter's. In October 2019 a new device model went in after a 7-month outage, and the level jumped about ×1.5 relative to other counters. The direction split and daily profile did not change. The open data cannot tell a real local change from a measurement change.

Open question for the Tiefbauamt

One answer would settle most of the range

Did the device change at Andreasstrasse in October 2019 (ECO → Y2H) alter what is counted, or did cycling routes around Oerlikon change in 2019–20? If it was a measurement change, the confidence falls to about 0.24 and "up about three-quarters" is the honest phrasing. If it was real, about 0.72 and "nearly doubled" holds.

    Read before quoting numbers

    Caveats

    • This is a retrospective spatial reconstruction using contemporaneous neighbour counts. It is not a forecast.
    • The network is today's OSM, including the Stadttunnel (opened 22 May 2025). Historical topology is not reconstructed, and travel time is a flat 16 km/h with no gradient.
    • City-only GBT scored 33.0 in the first run and 35.2 in the blend run, with the same seed, grid and validation sites. Something in the feature pipeline changed in between. The cantonal comparison therefore uses the two blend runs, which share code.
    • Cantonal stations use different sensors and site types. They enter as inputs and training sites with a source flag and are never scored.
    • Prediction intervals under-cover: 84% observed against 90% nominal with cantonal inputs, and 77% without.
    • The blend is biased low by about 10 passages per hour (−15% of the mean), and the bias is largest at peak hours.
